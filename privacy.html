Privacy Policy — Focused Reader

Effective date: 9 November 2026
App: Focused Reader (Android)
Developer: Eliáš Baltazár Špilák
Contact: elias.spilak@gmail.com

Focused Reader is a distraction-free, word-by-word reading app. It is built to be free to use, with ads, and an optional Pro subscription removes the ads. Your books and reading activity stay on your device. This policy explains what data does leave your device, and why, in plain language.

The short version: There are no accounts. Your library, bookmarks, and reading progress live only on your phone, and they are never sent to us or to ad companies. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects data such as your device's advertising ID and IP address. Pro subscribers get no ads. Data also leaves your device when you download a free book from an online catalog, when the app checks your subscription, and when the Meta (Facebook) SDK reports your advertising ID and certain app events, so we can measure the ads we run to promote the app. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. You have choices about all of this: see Your choices about ads and tracking below.

What we collect

Focused Reader has no user accounts, no login, and no profile. We do not ask for your name, email, or contacts. The app has no permission to access your device's location, and we do not collect your precise location. We run no servers of our own that receive your data. The companies we work with do receive some data, as explained below: Google for ads, Meta for measuring our own advertising, RevenueCat and Google Play for the subscription, and the online catalogs you choose to download from.

Your books and reading data (stays on your device)

The books you import or download, your bookmarks, your reading progress, and your app settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data, and it is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, including ad companies. The same goes for the small record the app keeps to decide when ads may appear, such as the time of the last full-screen ad and whether today's free download has been used. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it. You can back it up yourself using the app's export feature, which writes a file to a location you choose — that file stays under your control.

Files you open

When you import a book, the app reads the EPUB, PDF, or text file you select (or that you share to the app from another app) and processes it on your device to prepare it for reading. These files are not transmitted anywhere.

Downloading free books (network use)

The app lets you browse and download public-domain books from third-party online catalogs (for example, Project Gutenberg). When you use this feature, your device makes a normal internet request to those services. As with any web request, the remote server receives technical information such as your IP address; we do not send any personal information about you, and we have no control over those third parties' own practices. If you never use the online catalog, the app makes no such requests.

Subscriptions and payments

Focused Reader is free to use. The optional Pro subscription, which has a free trial, removes the ads. All payments are processed by Google Play — we never see or store your credit-card or payment details.

To determine whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service acting as our data processor. The app checks this for everyone, including people who have never subscribed. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier (not linked to your name or email) and basic technical information such as your device platform and app version, and, if you purchase or restore a subscription, your purchase receipt from Google Play. We use this solely to know whether you have Pro, which decides whether the app shows you ads. We do not use it to target ads, and we do not combine it with any other data.

Their handling of that data is governed by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Google's handling of your payment is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the app (Google AdMob)

The free version of Focused Reader shows ads provided by Google AdMob: banners on the library and Discover screens, full-screen ads when you open or close a book or import a file, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to download more books. There are no ads while you are reading. The ad service does not start during a new user's first reading session, and it does not start at all for Pro subscribers. Each time the app opens, it does ask Google whether consent rules apply to you, as described under If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland.

Once it is running, Google's ad software automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your device's general location.
  • Interactions with the app and its ads, including app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: your Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google encrypts this data in transit. As a result of serving ads, Google and other third parties, including ad vendors and networks, may place and read cookies or use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to collect information. They may use it to show you ads based on your interests, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure ads. We do not give them your books, reading activity, bookmarks, or file contents.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, ads depend on the choices you make in Google's consent form. If you do not consent, Google attempts to serve limited ads, which do not use personal data to personalize ads. IP addresses are still used to deliver the ad, and cookies or local storage may be used to detect invalid traffic and fraud.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps.

Measuring our own advertising (Meta)

To promote Focused Reader, we run advertising campaigns on Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram). So we can tell which of those ads actually lead to active readers and subscribers, the app includes Meta's app-events SDK. On Android it collects your device's advertising ID and other device data, and reports a small set of app events to Meta: that the app was installed and opened, that a new user opened their first book and finished their first reading session, that the Remove ads screen was viewed, and when a subscription is started (including the plan's price and currency). It does not report which books you read, your reading activity, bookmarks, highlights, or any file content.

Meta processes this data in accordance with the Meta Privacy Policy and its terms for business tools. We use it to measure and optimize our own advertising, and we do not combine it with any other data. This applies to Pro subscribers too. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, it runs only if you tap Allow when the app asks. Elsewhere, it starts when the app opens, and you can use the choices below.

Your choices about ads and tracking

  • In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland: under Settings → Privacy, Ad privacy options (free version) reopens Google's consent form, and Help us measure our ads turns sharing with Meta on or off. You can withdraw consent there at any time.
  • "Do not sell or share my info." Elsewhere, in the free version, turn this on in the app under Settings → Privacy. Ad requests then ask Google to apply restricted data processing, which limits how your data is used for ads. You will still see ads. This setting applies to Google's ads only, not to Meta.
  • Your advertising ID. In Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. This applies to both Google and Meta. With the ID deleted, there is no persistent advertising identifier for them to receive.
  • Google's ad settings. You can control ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Pro. A Pro subscription removes the ads, and the ad service does not start.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reading reminder, the notification is scheduled and shown entirely on your device. No reminder data is sent to us or any server.

Permissions the app may request

  • Files / storage — to let you pick and open the books you want to read, and to save a backup file where you choose.
  • Notifications — only if you enable the daily reading reminder.

You can decline or later revoke these in your device settings; the app simply disables the related feature. The app also declares the Android advertising ID permission, used as described above; you control the ID itself in your device settings.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data for money. We do share data with the third parties described above: Google for ads, Meta for measuring our advertising, online book catalogs you choose to download from, Google Play for payment processing, and RevenueCat for subscription status.

Some US state privacy laws define "sale" or "sharing" broadly enough to include letting advertising companies collect identifiers for targeted advertising. In that sense, the data described under Ads in the app and Measuring our own advertising may count as sold or shared. You can opt out as described under Your choices about ads and tracking.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on any server of our own, so there is generally nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or erase on request. Data collected by Google and Meta is held by them, and you can use the controls above and their own privacy tools to manage it. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, see the next section for your rights.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal data is collected, to request its correction or deletion, to opt out of its sale, its sharing, or its use for targeted advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. To use them, use the choices above or contact us at the address above.

You don't have to agree. Providing this data is not required by law or by any contract. The app works the same for reading whatever you choose.

Requesting deletion of your data

Focused Reader has no accounts and keeps your library, bookmarks, and reading progress only on your device. Data about you held elsewhere is of two kinds. The first is the anonymous subscription record kept by our processor, RevenueCat: a randomly generated identifier and, if you purchased or restored a subscription, your Google Play purchase receipt (see Subscriptions and payments above). It is not linked to your name or email. The second is data held by Google and Meta, described above, which they control and we cannot delete for you: use your Android advertising ID settings and their privacy tools, linked above.

To request deletion of that record, email elias.spilak@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request." We will delete the associated subscription record from RevenueCat and confirm by email, normally within 30 days. Note that deleting this record does not cancel an active subscription — you manage or cancel that in the Google Play Store — and any data stored locally on your device is removed whenever you uninstall the app.

Children

Focused Reader is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The free version contains ads, and the app is not meant for use by young children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the age at which you can give consent yourself depends on your country, and ranges from 13 to 16. If you are younger than that, ask a parent or guardian before agreeing.

Security

Because your reading data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We hold no server-side copy of it to secure or to breach.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
